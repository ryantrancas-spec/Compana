Resources

A curated set of trusted AI tools for UK teachers. Every tool listed here has been reviewed against UK government guidance on AI in education and is suitable for use within your school's AI policy.

✔
Policy Compliant Tools Only
All tools listed below align with the Department for Education's guidance on generative AI in education and support responsible, teacher-led use in the classroom. Always check your individual school's policy before use.
General AI Assistants

Powerful general-purpose AI tools that teachers use for planning, resource creation, drafting communications, and more. These are not built exclusively for education but are widely used and trusted across the profession.

ChatGPT
OpenAI
✔ Policy Compliant Check with school
The most widely used AI assistant in education. ChatGPT can help teachers draft lesson plans, generate quiz questions, explain concepts at different levels, summarise documents, and write feedback templates. It requires careful review of outputs before use — AI can produce plausible but inaccurate content.
Best used for
  • Drafting lesson plans and schemes of work
  • Generating differentiated explanations
  • Creating quiz or revision questions
  • Writing parent and admin communications
Things to be aware of
  • Always verify facts and subject accuracy
  • Do not input personal student data
  • Free tier available; advanced features require subscription
Visit ChatGPT →
Google Gemini
Google
✔ Policy Compliant Check with school
Google's AI assistant integrates directly with Google Workspace tools — Docs, Gmail, Slides, and Drive — making it especially useful for schools already using Google Classroom. Gemini can help generate content, summarise documents, and assist with planning without leaving the tools teachers already use daily.
Best used for
  • Drafting and editing within Google Docs
  • Summarising long documents or reports
  • Generating slide content in Google Slides
  • Research support integrated with Google Search
Things to be aware of
  • Best suited to schools already using Google Workspace
  • Review any AI-generated content before sharing with students
  • Data handling settings vary by school Google account configuration
Visit Gemini →
Education-Specific AI Tools

These tools are built specifically for UK schools — curriculum-aligned, designed with safeguarding in mind, and trusted across thousands of classrooms. They are the recommended starting point for teachers new to AI in education.

Oak National Academy — Aila
Oak National Academy
✔ Policy Compliant ★ Recommended Free
Aila is Oak National Academy's AI lesson assistant — a free tool designed specifically for UK teachers. It generates structured, curriculum-aligned lesson plans and resources based on your subject, year group, and learning objectives. As a government-supported organisation, Oak is one of the most trusted sources of AI-assisted teaching materials in England.
Best used for
  • Generating full lesson plans quickly
  • Creating curriculum-aligned activities and resources
  • Reducing lesson planning workload
  • Adapting content to different year groups
Why it's trusted
  • Backed by a UK government-supported organisation
  • Designed with teacher oversight built in
  • Outputs aligned to national curriculum standards
  • Free for all UK teachers
Visit Oak National Academy →
Sparx Learning
Sparx
✔ Policy Compliant ★ Recommended Often school-provided
Sparx uses AI to set personalised maths and reading homework that adapts to each student's level. It provides immediate feedback to students and detailed progress data to teachers — making it one of the most widely adopted AI-powered learning tools in UK secondary schools. Many schools subscribe at an institutional level.
Best used for
  • Setting structured, personalised weekly homework
  • Reinforcing classroom learning through practice
  • Tracking student engagement and completion
  • Identifying students who are struggling
Why it's trusted
  • Widely adopted across UK secondary schools
  • Built for classroom use with teacher oversight
  • Student data kept secure within school systems
  • Evidence-based approach to homework and practice
Visit Sparx Learning →
Tassomai
Tassomai
✔ Policy Compliant ★ Recommended Often school-provided
Tassomai is a UK-built AI revision platform that personalises practice using spaced repetition — a learning science technique proven to improve long-term retention. It identifies gaps in student knowledge and adapts question frequency accordingly. Widely used for GCSE preparation across Science, English, and other core subjects.
Best used for
  • Supporting GCSE revision across multiple subjects
  • Setting regular retrieval practice as homework
  • Identifying knowledge gaps before assessments
  • Improving long-term retention through spaced repetition
Why it's trusted
  • Built and based in the UK
  • Aligned to GCSE courses and specifications
  • Grounded in learning science research
  • Used across thousands of UK classrooms
Visit Tassomai →
