What's Inside

Everything a teacher needs to navigate AI confidently

Compana brings together personalised learning (specific to school policy, subject(s) taught and year group(s) taught, to ensure teachers can understand how to safely use and guide AI within their classroom in daily bites. We combine trusted resources and government guidance to ensure we help teachers stay compliant within their classroom AI usage.

🎓
Core Feature

AI Literacy Micro-Course

7 modules of bite-sized lessons that adapt to your subject, year groups and existing knowledge. A short diagnostic at the start skips content you already know — so you only learn what's actually useful to you.

  • Personalised to your subject and year groups
  • AI-generated lesson content tailored to your classroom
  • Skips lessons you've already mastered
  • Certificate of completion for each module
📋

School AI Policy Integration

Upload your school's AI policy and Compana will align all lesson content and tool recommendations to your specific guidelines — ensuring every teacher stays within your school's framework.

  • Upload any PDF or Word policy document
  • Content adapts to your school's rules
  • Includes a structured policy template if you don't have one yet
📚

Curated AI Resources

A hand-picked library of AI tools reviewed against UK government and school policy. Every tool is checked for DfE compliance, data safety, and classroom suitability — with personalised picks for your subject.

  • Policy-compliant tools only
  • Personalised recommendations for your subject
  • Direct links and guidance on when to use each tool
👥

Teacher Community

A shared space where teachers can ask questions, share tips, and get answers from the Compana team. Learn from what's working in real classrooms across the UK.

  • Ask questions and get expert answers
  • Share classroom tips with other teachers
  • Direct support from the Compana team
📊

Manager Dashboard

For senior leaders and heads of department — track your staff's progress through the AI literacy programme. See who's engaged, how long they're spending, and nudge those who need a reminder.

  • Track completion across your whole staff team
  • Lesson-by-lesson engagement data
  • Send reminder emails to inactive teachers
🏆

Certificates of Completion

Teachers earn a certificate for each module they complete — giving them a record of their AI professional development that they can share with their school leadership.

  • Certificate issued per module
  • Dated and timestamped
  • Suitable for CPD records
How It Works

From uncertain to confident in minutes

01

Take the Diagnostic

Answer 5 quick questions about your school, subject, year groups and existing AI knowledge. Takes under 2 minutes.

02

Get Your Learning Path

Compana builds a personalised curriculum for you — skipping what you already know and focusing on what you actually need.

03

Learn at Your Own Pace

Work through bite-sized lessons when it suits you. Each lesson takes 5-10 minutes and is packed with practical, classroom-relevant content.

04

Earn Your Certificate

Complete each module and receive a certificate — building a record of your AI professional development over time.
